# 🎬 Netflix India YouTube Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Netflix India's YouTube channel data (2016–2024)** using **Power BI** to uncover audience behavior, content performance, and publishing trends. The goal is to transform raw YouTube data into actionable business insights and strategic recommendations that help improve content reach and audience engagement.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

- Analyze Netflix India's YouTube channel performance.
- Identify factors influencing views and engagement.
- Discover the best publishing time, day, and video duration.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve channel growth.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes:

- Executive Summary
- Shorts vs Long-form Video Analysis
- Publishing Time Analysis
- Day-wise Performance
- Month & Year Trend Analysis
- Video Duration Analysis
- Tag Performance Analysis
- Strategic Recommendations

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- 📅 **September** had the highest number of published videos.
- 📅 **Thursday** was the most active publishing day.
- ⏰ Best publishing time:
  - Day: **9 AM – 11 AM**
  - Evening: **6 PM – 7 PM**
- 🎥 Videos between **5–10, 10–20, 20–40, and 60–120 minutes** achieved higher engagement.
- 🎬 **Bheem** content consistently ranked among the top-performing videos across both Shorts and Long-form.
- 📈 Engagement increased during **2020**, declined after the pandemic, and started recovering from **2023**.
- 🏷️ Most frequently used tags:
  - Netflix Shows
  - Netflix
  - Netflix India

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Publish promotional content during **9–11 AM** to maximize reach.
- Schedule engagement-focused content during **6–7 PM**.
- Continue producing high-performing content similar to the **Bheem** series.
- Focus on video durations that consistently deliver higher engagement.
- Optimize publishing schedules based on audience behavior and historical trends.
- Use popular tags consistently to improve discoverability.
